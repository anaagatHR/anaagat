## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a small inline script, no build step. The redesign lives in `index.html` + `styles.css` (+ `tokens.css`, `base.css`) and `assets/`. The original single-file preview `anaagat-website-preview (1) (1).html` is kept as the before reference.

## Users

- **Industries (primary):** owners, plant/site heads and HR or admin managers at manufacturing, warehousing & logistics, facility management, retail & e-commerce, construction, hospitality, healthcare and corporate/IT businesses. They need people hired, deployed, paid and kept compliant, often at short notice and across locations ("5 people next week or 500 across locations").
- **Training centres (primary):** skill institutes, colleges and training centres. They work with Anaagat in two ways: they **buy services** (payroll and compliance for their own staff) and they **host hiring drives and career seminars** run by Anaagat / RecruitKr for their trainees.
- Job seekers are **not** an audience of this site (removed at the owner's request). Candidate recruitment goes through RecruitKr.

## Product Purpose

Anaagat Humanpower is a workforce services company in Jaipur and the parent of RecruitKr. It runs payroll, statutory compliance, staffing, manpower and workforce deployment itself, and does permanent recruitment and executive search through RecruitKr. Success for the site = an employer or training centre requests a quote, calls, or books a seminar.

## Positioning

One team for the full employee cycle (hire, deploy, pay, comply) that owns the outcome, instead of a separate recruiter, contractor and payroll vendor.

## Operating Context

Indian labour-law context: PF, ESIC, PT, LWF, minimum wages, bonus, gratuity, CLRA, the New Labour Codes. Engagements start with a conversation about roles, shifts, budgets and compliance. A single account manager per engagement.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the employer's problem (people, pay, compliance), not with the company.
2. Every claim on the page is one the company can stand behind; no invented numbers.
3. One clear next step on every screen: quote, call, or book a seminar.
4. Respect for work: for the employer who depends on each hire and the worker whose livelihood it supports.
