Privacy policy

What we collect on this website, why, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

Anaagat Humanpower (“we”, “us”) runs anaagathr.com. Our corporate office is at 7/357, Sector 7, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302017. We decide how the personal data described here is used, so under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) we are its data fiduciary. RecruitKr, our group’s recruitment brand, runs its own website with its own policy.

What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote or consultation: your name, company or institute, email, mobile number, the service you are interested in and any comments you add.
  • When you subscribe to updates: your email address.
  • When you call or WhatsApp us: your number and what you choose to tell us.
  • When an Anaagat team member signs in: the name, email and profile picture of their company Google account. This applies only to our staff.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. Cloudflare, our hosting provider, processes this to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site sets a cookie for signed-in staff to keep them signed in, a short-lived security cookie during sign-in, and, only if you close the “available in Hindi” bar, a cookie that keeps it closed. Your choice of light or dark mode is saved in your own browser and is never sent to us. Our fonts load from Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry), and on our Hindi pages also from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts their servers when you open a page.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and deliver the services you ask for.
  • To send the updates you subscribed to. Every email has a way to unsubscribe.
  • To keep the website secure and working.
  • To meet our obligations under Indian law.

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you send it to us, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us; this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the website and communicate with you, such as Cloudflare (hosting and security) and Google (staff sign-in and Hindi fonts), Fontshare (fonts), under their data protection terms.
  • RecruitKr, our group company, when your enquiry is about recruitment and you have asked us to help with it.
  • Government authorities, courts or regulators when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it for, or as long as the law requires us to keep business records. After that we delete it.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent;
  • handle a grievance about how we have processed it;
  • let a person you nominate exercise these rights if you die or cannot act yourself.

Write to our Grievance Officer (details below). We will reply within the time the law sets. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How we protect it

The website runs over HTTPS. Staff areas need a company Google account. Access to enquiries is limited to the people who need it to serve you.

Children

This website is for businesses and institutions. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Our blog

Blog posts are drafted with the help of AI from public news reports, then checked and published by our team. Writing them does not use any visitor’s personal data.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be shown on the home page for a reasonable time.
